Materials & Product Care

Where is the jewelry made?

All Sonasonsproducts are designed and manufactured in our own factory.

What metal is used in the jewelry?

We only use Swiss pure gold with master alloys. For 18 karat, 75% is pure Swiss gold and remaining 25% is the master alloy that gives color to the gold. In case of 14 Karat, 58.5% is pure Swiss gold and remaining 41.5% is the master alloy that gives color to the gold.

Where are the diamonds sourced from?

We use only the best within your budget and that is why we offer HI-SI and GH-VS quality diamonds. We have the best source for diamonds and all our diamonds are sorted and graded for its consistency and later the final product is certified by a third part laboratory like SGL, GIN, GIH & IGI for its authenticity.

Do you provide a certificate of authenticity?

You can rely on us that each piece comes with its own Certificate of Authenticity . Your diamond jewellery comes with a certification from GIH/GIN/SGL for its authenticity of the diamonds and BIS hallmarked for the purity of gold.

Is my jewelry hallmarked?

Your jewelry is BIS hallmarked for the purity of gold.

What is the quality of your stones?

All the diamonds are natural and graded as per GIA (Gemological Institute of America) standards.

What is HI-SI and GH-VS?

GH & HI represent the color standard and VS & SI represent the clarity of diamonds.

How do I take care of my jewelry?

When our pieces are not worn, we recommend storing them separately in a lined box provided or in soft pouch, so that they don’t rub together or become tangled. This helps prevent scratching and maintains color.. You can avoid applying scents, lotions and sprays near to your jewelry, alternatively it is advisable to put your jewelry on last or before heading out.
