What is the policy for return or exchange?

We want you to be satisfied with our products, so returning a piece is made extremely easy. Try your purchases in the comfort of your home and if you are not satisfied, we will be happy to offer you an exchange or refund within 10 days from the delivery date. Complete refund shall be given to the customer post review from our QA team. This includes pieces purchased in the sale or with a promotion code. Please make sure that all original tags are attached, original packaging and certification are intact and there are no signs of wear or tear on any of the jewellery pieces. Our QA team will review the returned product and we will get back to you. Kindly keep in mind that engraved pieces or customized products are not eligible for return or exchange In case, any coupon, discount or promo codes were used during the original purchase, the exchange amount will be reduced by an amount equivalent to the coupon, discount or promo codes, as applicable. All returns must be accompanied by a copy of your proof of purchase. In case, the customer cannot produce the original certificate, the company will send the diamond jewellery to the lab for recertification, the shipping and certification cost of which shall be borne by the customer. Sonasons is not responsible for lost or stolen items once declared ‘Delivered / Received’ by the courier service. We are unable to exchange any items once they have been altered by another jeweler. The same is no longer considered a product of Sonasons. Sonasons reserves the right to reject returns from any particular customer, in case of frequent returns by such customer, which shall mean returns on more than 2 occasions in a financial year.

In case the exchanged value is higher than the value of the old product, the customer will have to pay the difference between the new product chosen and the amount paid towards the old product. In case the exchanged value is lesser than the value of the old product, the difference will be held as a credit for future purchases.
